SAMPLE — JDM VINTAGE WATCHES

A median can hide the whole gap

The Seiko 6309 sold in Japan for ¥35,000 in 2019 — and still ¥35,000 in 2026. In the US the same watch doubled, $350 to $700, over the same years. But the Grand Seiko SBGM221 shows the trap: Japan-side prices for the same reference span ¥35,500 to ¥691,500. A single median for that reference (¥154,000) tells you nothing. This sample shows four references with Japan-side prices measured from public listings in November 2026. FX: ¥159.06 / $1.

The honest part: only one of the four rows is a real comparison — the Seiko 6309, where the Japan price stayed flat at ¥35,000 while the US price doubled, a 15–50% gap that has held since 2021 with no sign of converging. The SBGM221 row is the warning built into this dataset: the same reference in Japan spans ¥35,500 to ¥691,500, so its ¥154,000 median is not a price — and no gap is claimed from it even though the US sold median is $4,200. The SBGX083 row has one US sale; one sale is not a market. And even the 6309 row is gross only: eBay fees, US duty and shipping are not deducted yet, so the WATCH is not a buy signal. Any feed that compares medians of unmatched watches is not a price feed.

How we compare — the matched-pair rule

  • Reference: brand + reference number must match exactly. Near matches are not compared.
  • Condition: same condition, same accessories (box, papers) and same seller type (private / shop) — a row never mixes them.
  • Medians: never used as a gap on their own. A median is only shown with its spread, and a row with a 19× Japan-side spread claims no gap at all.
  • Thin data: US sold counts are always shown. A reference with one sale is labelled “1 sale only”.
  • Collection: manual snapshots of public listings. Mercari blocks bots, so Japan-side prices are hand-checked (same handling as TCG's Mercari source).

HOW WE COMPARE

Same reference, same condition, same accessories — or no gap

Watch prices swing with condition and accessories, so a comparison is only valid when all of them match at once.

  • Reference: brand + reference number must match exactly. Near matches are not compared.
  • Condition: same condition grade, same box/papers status, same seller type. A “no-box” watch is a different item from a “full-set” watch of the same reference.
  • Medians: a median without its spread is not a price. Wide-spread references claim no gap.
  • Sources: public retail and marketplace listings only. No member-only auction data, no scraped private marketplaces.
