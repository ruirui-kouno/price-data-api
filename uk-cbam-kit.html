WHY THE DEADLINES MATTER

Day one means money — not paperwork

The EU gave importers a transitional reporting period before any money changed hands. The UK did not. From 1 January 2027, every covered import carries a CBAM charge, and the charge is a tax administered by HMRC — not a certificate system like the EU. Complying with the EU scheme does not satisfy the UK one, and vice versa. If you trade with both, you need both answers.

The UK scheme is a tax, not the EU certificate system — do not mix the two. Rules may still change before commencement, and the kit will be updated when they move.

The honest part: this kit does not do your emissions measurement or your supplier survey for you — that work stays with your company. What it does is give you the templates, the calculation method and the checklists so you can work out whether you are in scope, register, and prepare the evidence HMRC will ask for, in English, without starting from a blank page.
