WHY THE DEADLINES MATTER

The cost of using default values is real money

Under CBAM, importers must buy and surrender certificates for the embedded emissions of what they import. If you cannot supply measured emissions data, default values apply — and defaults mean more certificates. Measured data costs less. That is why the data itself has a price.
