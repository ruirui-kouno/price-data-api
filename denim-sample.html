SAMPLE — JAPANESE SELVEDGE DENIM

The Japan–US gap, one pair at a time

Japanese selvedge denim retails for far less in Japan than abroad — but only when the same SKU, the same size and the same condition are compared. This sample shows three models where both sides of the price could be confirmed from public sources on Nov 24, 2026. New condition only. FX: ¥158.70 / $1.

Japan retail vs. US retail, same model

The honest part: the gross gap (31–54% at list prices) mostly evaporates once costs are deducted. After the 13.6% eBay fee, 16.6% US duty and ¥5,300 EMS shipping, only one row clears $30 net — Momotaro 0705SP, and only at the cheapest Japan price (¥22,000). Iron Heart IH-634S stays at +¥3,247 (WATCH), and Momotaro 0405SP is negative at its listed Japan price. Three more models were checked and left out because only one side of the price could be confirmed (Iron Heart IH-633S US $370, IH-634S-142 US $335, Pure Blue Japan XX-013 US $292–$326 — Japan-side price not yet confirmed). We do not publish a row until both sides are in hand.

Fee schedule used for the net figures (Nov 24, 2026)

  • eBay final value fee: 13.6% of the total sale (clothing, no store) + $0.40 per order. Verified against eBay's 2026 fee pages.
  • US import duty: 16.6% MFN, HTS 6203.42 (men's cotton denim jeans). Applied on the US sale value; duty is prepaid by the sender under the US-bound mail rules in force since October 2026.
  • Shipping: Japan Post EMS 1 kg to the US, ¥5,300 (official rate, valid until Jan 31, 2027; rises to ¥7,400 from Feb 1, 2027).
  • Duty stack — resolved Nov 24, 2026: MFN 16.6% only. The possible extra +10% baseline duty (Section 122) expired on Jul 24, 2026 after its 150-day statutory limit (Congress did not extend it; confirmed across Zonos, TariffOS and ustariffrates.com). The Section 301 forced-labour duty that took effect the same day puts Japan in a 12.5% combined frame (additional = 12.5% − MFN, floored at 0) — since denim's MFN rate of 16.6% exceeds 12.5%, the additional duty is 0%. The earlier warning scenario (“+10% would remove every ALERT”) no longer applies; 0705SP at ¥22,000 keeps its ALERT on the tariff side. Note: the Section 301 duty itself is under litigation (25 state attorneys general sued on Aug 3, 2026; CBP continues to collect), so the stack can still move — we will re-check it on each update. The duty-free mail threshold (DDP prepay) was raised from $800 to $2,500 on Jul 24, 2026.
  • Not included: Japan-side purchase tax/shipping, returns risk, currency conversion spread, and any proxy or listing fees. The net shown is therefore still an upper bound, not a guaranteed margin.

HOW WE COMPARE

Same SKU, same size, same condition — or no row

Denim prices swing with size and wear, so a comparison is only valid on three axes at once.

  • Model (SKU): brand + model code + fabric weight + finish must match exactly. Near matches are not compared.
  • Size: waist × length, tag size as listed. Sanforized / one-washed vs. unsanforized are different items. Size-specific prices are verified before a model moves from sample to feed.
  • Condition: New only in this stage. Used jeans are graded separately (New / Like New / Light Fade / Heavy Fade) and never mixed with new in the same table.
  • Sources: public retail listings only. No member-only auction data, no scraped private marketplaces.
